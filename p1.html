Balaghat Highlights:

Balaghat is a district in the state of Madhya Pradesh, India. It's known for its rich cultural heritage and natural beauty. The district is famous for its forests, which are part of the Kanha National Park, a major tiger reserve. Balaghat is also known for its manganese ore deposits.
